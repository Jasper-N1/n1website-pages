Healthcare, from the first-person perspective.

Logo

An app icon and a lowercase wordmark.

The icon is "n1" in a brand-blue rounded square with a 20% corner. On the blue canvas it reverses to paper. The wordmark is "n1.care", always lowercase: a bold "n1", a matcha dot, then "care", as on the staging app. The green logo is still in the logo kit for material that uses it.

Typography

A serif for the voice, a sans for the interface, a mono for the data.

Three families, no more. Tight tracking only at large sizes; wide tracking only on small uppercase labels.

Motion

Scattered data gathers into one clear record.

The canvas does not draw a heart. Two soft light shapes behind the blue pulse in a double beat, one cycle every 0.882 s, which is 68 bpm.
