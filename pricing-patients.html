Pricing FAQ

Questions about patient pricing.

How personal allowances work, what gets counted and how your plan stays separate from your doctor's.

Ask us another question →
Can I try n1 before choosing a plan?

Yes. The free trial includes 50 processed pages and one generated report, with no card required.

What counts as a processed page?

A page counts when you choose to begin extraction. Simply adding a health file does not use your allowance, and n1 shows you the page count before processing begins.

What counts as a generated report?

One report is counted each time you create a full or focused report. Opening or reviewing a report you have already generated does not create another report.

Do I pay for work my doctor does?

No. Your patient allowance is separate from a clinician's. Work your doctor performs through their own account draws on their allowance, not yours.

Does the number of doctors I see affect my price?

No. Patient pricing is based on the pages you process and the reports you create, not the number of doctors or specialists you visit.

Which plan should I choose?

Start with the approximate number of pages in the records you want n1 to read, then consider how many reports you expect to create each month. If you are unsure, we can help estimate your usage.

Are the displayed prices and terms final?

The patient figures are currently indicative. We will show or confirm the current price, applicable tax, allowance and renewal terms before you subscribe.
