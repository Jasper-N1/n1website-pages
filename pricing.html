Pricing FAQ

Questions about clinician pricing.

Clear answers on allowances, patient volume and plans for larger teams.

Ask us another question →
What counts as a processed page?

A page counts when you choose to begin extraction. Simply adding a file does not use your allowance, and n1 shows you the page count before processing begins.

What counts as a generated report?

One report is counted each time you create a full or focused report. Opening or reviewing a report you have already generated does not create another report.

Are allowances shared across my patients?

Yes. The page and report allowances belong to the clinician account and are shared across all of that clinician's patients. The number of patients itself is not metered.

Can multiple doctors use one plan?

The plans shown here are for one doctor account. If you need access for a practice or a larger team, talk to us about an enterprise plan.

Is white-label branding included?

White-label branding is an optional add-on to a paid plan. It lets you adapt the patient experience with your organisation's name, colours and identity.

Can I try n1 before choosing a paid plan?

Yes. The free trial includes 50 processed pages and one generated report, with no card required.

Are the displayed prices and terms final?

Not yet. You cannot buy a clinician plan on this website today. We confirm the current plan, price, applicable tax and terms with you directly before you sign up.
