Patient FAQ

Questions about using n1.

Short answers about your records, reports, sharing, and privacy.

Ask us another question →
What health records can I upload?

You can upload health records as PDFs and supported TXT, CSV, or TSV files. These include laboratory and imaging reports, specialist letters, clinical notes, medication lists, and genetic reports provided as readable documents. n1 extracts relevant facts such as dates, results, units, reference ranges, diagnoses, medications, supplements, and procedures. Raw genetic-data files are not supported.

What happens after I upload a record?

n1 checks whether the file can be processed, reads its contents, and identifies the document type, dates, and relevant health information. Extracted results and other facts are organized into your longitudinal record so information from different sources can be viewed together. Each item remains connected to the original document and page, allowing you to verify it in context before using it in a report or sharing it.

Can I check information against the original document?

Yes. You can open the evidence behind an extracted result and review the original document and page alongside available details such as the date, unit, and reference range. If something does not match the source, correct it before relying on the structured record. Checking the source is especially important before discussing a result with a clinician or sharing a generated report.

Does n1 diagnose conditions or replace my doctor?

No. n1 organizes information, highlights changes, and prepares draft summaries to make health records easier to review. It is not a substitute for a qualified clinician and should not be used on its own to diagnose a condition, assess an emergency, or choose or change treatment. Discuss results, symptoms, and care decisions with an appropriate healthcare professional; seek urgent medical help when necessary.

Can my doctor automatically see everything I upload?

No. Your patient workspace is private by default. A clinician receives access only when you share a report, accept an invitation, or create another connection using the controls shown in the product. Review the scope of access before confirming it, and remove or disable access when it is no longer needed. Connecting with one clinician does not automatically make your entire record available to every clinician you see.

How do I share a report?

Create or open the report, read the full contents, and check important statements against their linked sources. When it is ready, choose the sharing option and select the intended recipient or secure-link settings available to you. Only share the information needed for that conversation. You can review active access and disable a shared link when it is no longer required.

How is my health information protected?

Files are encrypted while being transferred and while stored, and access is limited through authenticated accounts and sharing controls. You can hide identifying information, process a file without retaining the original source, choose a storage region, and control access to individual reports. No online service can eliminate every risk, so use a secure device, protect your account credentials, and review access regularly. Read the Privacy Notice for details about collection, use, retention, and your choices.

Can I try n1 before creating a full account?

Yes. Open the live demo without signing up to explore a sample patient record, inspect source-linked results, and see how reports are presented. The demo uses sample information rather than your own health records. When you are ready to upload and organize your own files, create a free account and review the applicable privacy information and terms.
