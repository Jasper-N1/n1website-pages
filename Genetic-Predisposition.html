12 · Report basis

Methods and limitations

This review covered 116 served genetic conclusions together with available biomarkers, diagnoses, procedures, medications, and supplements. Raw-DNA calls are screening-grade and require clinical confirmation. Biomarkers were interpreted using the laboratory’s authoritative range status.

Screening evidence only

Susceptibility alleles indicate tendencies rather than diagnoses. Metabolizer phenotypes require complete validated haplotypes. Normal biomarkers do not exclude future disease or structurally present disease that is biochemically silent. Limitations include incomplete variant coverage, absent phasing, treatment-modified measurements, and phenotype tests that may be insensitive to early disease.
