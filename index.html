Technical safeguards

TLS 1.3 for data in transitTraffic between the browser and n1 is encrypted with TLS 1.3. Database connections must also use TLS. AES-256 for stored dataSource records and uploaded files are encrypted at rest with AES-256. Credentials kept in AWS Secrets ManagerAPI keys and service credentials stay out of the codebase. Services receive them only at runtime. Managed WAF and DDoS protectionIncoming requests pass through managed firewall rules and denial-of-service protection before they reach n1 services. Approved accounts with 2FAAccess to internal tools is limited to approved work accounts. A second factor is required at sign-in. Production kept separateProduction and staging run in separate environments. AI processing uses dedicated production infrastructure.
