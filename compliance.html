AI & data use

Clear rules for AI

No training

Your data does not train AI

n1.care does not use Customer Patient Data to train general-purpose AI models and does not permit AI providers to do so.

Minimum data

Only what the task needs

The AI services n1.care uses receive only the information a task needs, and are not allowed to keep it or learn from it.

Human review

A clinician decides

Qualified human review is required before an output is used in care. n1.care supports documentation and preparation — it never makes the clinical decision and is not a diagnostic device.

Security FAQ

Questions about data protection.

Plain answers about where records go, who can see them, and how to remove them.

Who can access patient records?

The clinician who uploads records and the patients they share with. Behind the scenes, only the few n1.care staff who run the service can reach patient data, and every access is logged. The AI services n1.care uses see only what a task needs and never keep it.

Is patient data used to train AI models?

No. n1.care does not use Customer Patient Data to train general-purpose AI models and does not permit AI providers to use that data to train their general-purpose models.

Read the AI and training section in our Privacy Notice →

Where is data stored — does it leave my country?

When you create an account, you choose where your uploaded records are stored: United States, EU (Frankfurt), United Kingdom (London), Singapore, India (Mumbai) or Australia (Sydney). Each patient can have their own location. Information extracted from those records, generated reports and account data are stored in the United States, as described in the Privacy Notice.

Do I need patient consent before uploading records?

That depends on your jurisdiction and role: as the party providing the records, you are responsible for having the authority to do so — through consent, treatment relationship, or another lawful basis your rules recognize.

Can n1.care run on-premises or self-hosted?

No — n1.care is delivered as a managed cloud service.

Is n1.care HIPAA compliant?

Yes. n1.care is built and operated to meet HIPAA requirements, and the safeguards on this page apply to every account. Where your practice needs one, n1.care signs a Business Associate Agreement with paid accounts. Trial accounts are not covered, so put the agreement in place before patient records go in.
